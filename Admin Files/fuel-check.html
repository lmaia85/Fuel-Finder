How much do you actually need?

Most people under-fuel long events by a wide margin. This works backwards from your finish time to a per-hour target, then tells you what that looks like in sachets.

Where the numbers come from. Product data via the Open Food Facts open database, which is crowdsourced and explicitly not guaranteed accurate — check anything that looks wrong against the pack. Dose ranges come from a reference file in this page, not from the model, so the same product gives the same answer twice.

Educational only. Not medical or nutritional advice. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or take medication that interacts with caffeine or sodium, talk to a professional. Practise race fuelling in training — gut tolerance is trainable and race morning is a poor time to find out.
